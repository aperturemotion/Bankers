WHAT CHANGED: 6 lines.
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- "Tesla built a car that flies over other cars."
    -> "Tesla built a car that jumps over other cars."
    [medium] The move over the cars was a thruster HOP: CNN 'jumped over a line of existing Tesla models', AP 'short airborne hops', TechCrunch 'lift itself over vehicles'. And the film's own honest part says the FAA does not count it as an aircraft ('The Tesla vehicle does not meet the FAA definition of an aircraft', FAA to CBS) — 'flies' in our own voice would contradict it. 'Flying car' stays Tesla's and Musk's word, not ours.

- "And Elon Musk calls it the last of the human-driven cars."
    -> "And Elon Musk calls it the best of the last human-driven cars."
    [high] Musk's words were 'the best of the last of the human-driven cars' (TechCrunch, Oct 15, 2026; AP: 'best of the last human-driven cars'). Dropping 'best of' turns his claim into a different one — that it IS the last human-driven car. A quotation keeps its meaning.

- "Then compressed-air thrusters, built with SpaceX, lifted it over a row of other Teslas."
    -> "Then compressed-air thrusters, developed with SpaceX, lifted it over a row of other Teslas."
    [low] Reuters: 'developed in collaboration with SpaceX'; TechCrunch quotes Musk on 'Tesla and SpaceX Engineers working closely together'. Who BUILT the thrusters is not reported. 'Then' holds: the hop over the cars was a second display after the ramp flip (CNN, The National News Desk).

- "Tesla says it goes from zero to sixty in one second."
    -> "Tesla says it goes from zero to sixty in under one second."
    [medium] AP: Tesla said it can go 'from zero to 60 miles … an hour in less than a second'; CNN: Musk said 'less than a second'; TechCrunch calls the Thrust trim 'sub-one-second'. 'In one second' understates the claim and is not what Tesla said.

- "Musk has said both companies set up committees to discuss a merger."
    -> "Musk has said both companies set up committees to look at a possible merger."
    [low] Musk on SpaceX's Q3 call, Oct 20, 2026 (TechCrunch, NBC Boston): both boards formed special committees; no agreement on terms. Tesla's own 10-Q (Oct 22, 2026, Part II Item 5): its special committee is to 'explore deepening Tesla's relationship with SpaceX, which could be through … a business combination'. A merger is one possibility under study, not the committees' set agenda.

- "Production isn't planned until twenty twenty-eight, and Musk admits success isn't guaranteed."
    -> "Production isn't planned until twenty twenty-eight."
    [medium] 2028 holds (CBS, AP, TechCrunch). But Musk's 'success is not guaranteed' (CBS/AFP) was about whether that night's LIVE STUNT would work — said while explaining why no one was in the car, followed by 'It'll probably work' (CNN). Beside the production date it reads as a warning about the car's future, which he did not give. Cut; the honest part keeps the FAA, the 2028 date and the unagreed merger.
