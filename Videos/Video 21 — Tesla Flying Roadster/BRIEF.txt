Musk has said both companies set up committees to discuss a merger.

[THE HONEST PART]
Now, the honest part.
The FAA says the Roadster doesn't meet its definition of an aircraft.
Production isn't planned until twenty twenty-eight, and Musk admits success isn't
guaranteed.
And no merger terms have been agreed.

[AUDIENCE QUESTION]
So be honest.
Is Tesla still a car company?
Tell us below.
