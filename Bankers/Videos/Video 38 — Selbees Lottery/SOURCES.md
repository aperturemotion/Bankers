# Video 37 — Theranos: SOURCES (checked 2026-11-24)

- [SEC complaint, SEC v. Holmes et al., 5:18-cv-01602](https://www.sec.gov/litigation/complaints/2018/comp-pr2018-41-theranos-holmes.pdf) — March 14, 2018. ¶4 "a few drops of blood from a finger"; ¶5 ~12 of 200+ tests on the proprietary analyzer; ¶13 founded 2003; ¶15 left college in her second year; ¶37 the Sept 2013 Walgreens launch ("as little as a few drops"); ¶54 "runs comprehensive blood tests from a finger-stick"; ¶2/¶47 raised more than $700M.
- SEC press release 2018-41 — March 14, 2018. "The vast majority of patient tests" on others' analyzers; Holmes's $500,000 penalty and 10-year bar.
- [DOJ case page, US v. Elizabeth Holmes et al.](https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndca/us-v-elizabeth-holmes-et-al) — the device names "the TSPU, Edison, or minilab".
- [Fortune, Roger Parloff, "This CEO is out for blood"](https://fortune.com/2014/06/12/theranos-blood-holmes/) — June 12, 2014. "More than $9 billion"; "just a few drops of blood"; born February 1984; Real-Time Cures; dropped out "a semester later".
- [Fortune, Theranos board of directors](https://fortune.com/2014/06/12/theranos-board-directors/) — June 12, 2014. Shultz (joined July 2011), Kissinger.
- Fortune — October 24, 2015 (41 Theranos Wellness Centers, mostly Phoenix); October 29, 2015 (board of counselors); December 17, 2015 (Parloff, "How Theranos misled me": the slogan "One tiny drop changes everything").
- Walgreens press release — June 12, 2016. "Terminating its relationship and closing operations at all 40 Theranos Wellness Centers at its stores in Arizona."
- The Wall Street Journal, John Carreyrou — October 15, 2015 (the first report; the WSJ host refused the fetch, read via same-day Fortune/TechCrunch coverage); May 18, 2016 (two years of Edison results voided, via TechCrunch May 18 and Fortune May 19).
- [CMS imposition of sanctions on Theranos](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/CMS_Imposition_of_Sanctions_on_Theranos,_Inc.) — July 7, 2016 (Jan 25, 2016 "immediate jeopardy").
- KQED — November 17, 2016. George Shultz a board member 2011–15; Tyler Shultz's role.
- [US Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit, US v. Holmes, No. 22-10312, amended opinion](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2025/12/22/23-1166.pdf) — December 22, 2025. p.16: the counts (4 investor counts guilty, patient counts acquitted, 3 hung), 135 months; p.17: $452M restitution (May 2023).
- [AP via PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/on-third-day-of-testimony-elizabeth-holmes-admits-mistakes-but-denies-deception) — November 24, 2021. Her testimony: she "never stopped believing" in the technology.
- Bloomberg — March 26, 2026; Reason/Volokh — March 27, 2026. The sentence cut from 135 to 123 months.
- [ABC7 News](https://abc7news.com/story/elizabeth-holmes-scheduled-move-halfway-house-august-2027/19862832/) — September 24, 2026. Halfway house Aug 23, 2027 (not used on screen).
- [JAMA, Ioannidis, "Stealth Research"](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2110977) — February 17, 2015. No peer-reviewed Theranos data as of January 5, 2015.
- [Bioengineering & Translational Medicine 3(1):58–70](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5773944) — January 2018. The later peer-reviewed paper (why the line says "at its peak", not "never").
