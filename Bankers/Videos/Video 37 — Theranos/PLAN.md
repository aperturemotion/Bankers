CHANGED FROM THE BRIEF (and why):
1. Hook: "One drop of blood" → "A few drops of blood" — "one tiny drop" was the ad slogan; the actual claim (2013 Walgreens release,
   SEC complaint ¶4, Fortune 2014) was "a few drops".
2. "In 2003, nineteen-year-old Elizabeth Holmes dropped out of Stanford and founded Theranos" → founded it in 2003 at nineteen, THEN
   dropped out (SEC ¶15; Fortune 2014: she dropped out "a semester later").
3. "a small machine called the Edison" → "its Edison machine" (length; same claim).
4. "more than forty stores" → "about forty" (Fortune Oct 2015: 41; Walgreens June 2016: 40 in Arizona).
5. The crack: "But most tests weren't run on the Edison. They ran on ordinary machines from other companies." → "But most tests ran on
   other companies' machines, not the Edison." (some of those machines were modified — "ordinary" dropped; SEC ¶5).
6. "The whistleblower was Tyler Shultz, the grandson of her own board member" → "One whistleblower was Tyler Shultz, a board member's
   grandson" (Erika Cheung and Adam Rosendorff were sources too).
7. "broke the story" → "exposed it" (length).
8. "In 2018, the company shut down" → "In 2018, it shut down" (length).
9. Verdict: "In January 2022, Holmes was found guilty … and sentenced to eleven years and three months" → the sentence came in
   November 2022, and a judge cut it by 12 months on March 26, 2026 (to 123 months): "Her sentence: eleven years and three months,
   later cut by one year."
10. "she was cleared of the charges about defrauding patients" → its own sentence, "And she was cleared of defrauding patients."
11. Lesson: "Theranos never published its data for outside scientists to check" → "At its peak, Theranos had published no peer-reviewed
   data …" — a peer-reviewed paper did appear in January 2018 (Bioengineering & Translational Medicine), so "never" is false; at its
   peak (Ioannidis, JAMA, Feb 2015) there was none.
12. Length: the brief's lines spelled out ran 215 words (≈ 82 s of voice, over the 80-s cap); trimmed to 186 with no claim cut.
13. On screen: no photos of Holmes, the Edison or the WSJ front page (the brief asked for real photos; the house rules ban people and
