## 2. Hook (0–3 s) and ending (last 5 s)

**Hook.** The film starts on a relatable, first-person action — you, behind the wheel, flashing your headlights at the light (stylised hands on the wheel, frame 0 caption "Flashing", 0.5 s light flare). By 1.5 s it dissolves to the "little camera" and by 4.5 s the myth is killed ("does absolutely nothing."). The rule: frame 0 = the viewer's own POV doing the wrong thing; 4.5 s = myth debunked; 6.5 s = the teaser ("It's listening for something else.").

**Ending (82–87.07 s).** Payoff + practical tip: the bike sits on the cut line and the lights turn green at ≈83.5 s (visual reward); the painted bike stencil appears on the loop (85.5 s). Follow card fades in at 85.0 s top centre (dark rounded card, round yellow logo, "SizedUnderstood @sizedunderstood", yellow "Follow" pill); a cursor moves in from the right, clicks ≈85.9 s → outlined "Following". Last caption "a little bike there." with *bike* boxed. VO ends 86.38 s, ≈0.7 s music tail (≈ -42 dBFS RMS). No end card.
